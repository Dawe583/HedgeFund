# F1 ledger verdict — the trend family, in-sample screening complete

Date: 2026-11-26. Family F1 (time-series momentum), phase A complete:
80/80 cells REJECTED, zero errors, the full grid as declared in
`trading/candidates.py`. Rows in `backend/data/research.db:search_ledger`
ids 1–80 — the ledger's first family, evaluated end-to-end in 45 seconds
of wall time on 8 workers. This document is the family's reading, per
program-2026-11-26 §2: failures recorded with the same care as passes.

## The headline

**No phase-B candidate exists — but the family did not fail the way noise
fails.** The top cell (`trendmom-L84-e1.5-x1.0` on ETHUSDT) passes three
of the gate's four checks — permutation p 0.021 < 0.05, bootstrap CI lower
+0.393 > 0, walk-forward 4/5 — and fails the fourth structurally: 13
trades where 30 are required. That is not a near-miss to iterate on; it
is the family's frequency ceiling showing.

## What the distribution says

Failure shapes across 80 cells: walk-forward consistency 0–2/5 in 60 of
them, trade counts of 4–16 in most of the rest. The two shapes are one
shape: slow momentum at 4h over 23 months of in-sample tape cannot trade
often, full stop. Momentum that flips at z-reversal exits and re-enters
(the harness has no reversal — flips are exit-then-re-entry by design)
produces 4–16 round trips in two years per symbol. The gate's ≥30-trades
floor exists because fewer trades cannot carry the statistics; for this
family per-symbol, the floor is unreachable by construction at any
lookback that is still "trend".

The validated-statistics shape IS present in the family: the top quintile
of cells (L84/L168, entry_z 1.5–2.0) consistently shows positive Sharpe
(0.8–1.2), mostly-positive CI lowers, walk-forward 3–4/5. Momentum at
weekly lookbacks on ETH and BTC in-sample was real — the evidence is the
ordering significance at p 0.021, not a vibe. What is missing is the
sample size, and sample size is structural.

## The structural finding, written into the program

**Per-symbol validation is the wrong shape for slow families.** A slow
edge validates as a basket composite — the same signal fired across ten
independent coins aggregates to statistical significance no single coin
can carry. That is F3's design, and F1's verdict makes it the load-bearing
machinery: without basket validation, every slow family (F1, F2 funding
carry) is dead before it starts, not because the edges are absent but
because the measurement cannot see them. The work order changes: basket
composite machinery moves ahead of F5 implementation.

What F1 does not justify: parameter iteration. The grid was spent as
declared, the survivors that matter are structural, and re-running the
same family with tweaked thresholds is exactly the multiple-testing leak
the program exists to prevent. F1 is closed for per-symbol; its hypothesis
survives into the basket machinery as-is, grid intact, ledger ancestry
honest.

## Second-order evidence

- **ETH trends harder than BTC in-sample.** The top cells on ETHUSDT
  consistently out-sharpe their BTCUSDT twins at identical parameters
  (1.19 vs 0.85 at the L84-e1.5 frontier). Altcoin trend premia being
  larger than BTC's is literature-consistent; it also previews why the
  basket matters — edge lives in the tail coins, not the majors.
- **Rails-off screening cost nothing here:** the kill-switch would have
  censored several mid-grid cells below even their 4–16 trades. The
  two-mode decision in the program is validated by usage, not argument.
- **45 seconds for 80 full-gate evaluations** (kernel harness + 1000
  permutations + 1000 bootstraps each, 8 workers) means compute is free
  at this scale. The binding constraint of the whole program is statistical
  honesty, never compute — the grid sizes are chosen by governance, not
  runtime.
