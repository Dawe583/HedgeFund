# The search for the first validated edge — program and governance

Date: 2026-11-26. Status: proposed governance, data expansion already running.
Companion to `docs/design/hedge-fund-os.md` §11 ("No proven edge exists… the
actual work starts: finding one strategy that passes. That work is research,
not engineering") and the M5 audit's missing-item 1. This document is the
research half of that sentence: what we search, over what data, and — the
part that decides whether a pass means anything — the selection discipline.

## 1. What we know (measured, not assumed)

- 16/16 production-parameter cells REJECTED over 365 days of BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT
  1h+4h, verdicts invariant across two engines and the rails landing
  (`kernel-sweep-2026-11-26.md`). The catalog as it stands has no edge at
  production parameters; nothing about the fill model or rails is hiding one.
- Nearest misses and their shapes:
  - `hft BTCUSDT 4h`: PF 1.71, Sharpe 1.60, wf 4/5 — but permutation p 0.41.
    The ordering of its trades carries no information; magnitude without
    ordering significance is luck's favorite disguise.
  - `meanrev BTCUSDT 4h`: permutation p 0.011 — but bootstrap CI lower −1.58
    and wf 1/5. Ordering significance with a negative expectation floor is
    noise dressed as signal.
  Reading: the gate's redundancy is the product. Each test alone is
  beatable by chance at moderate sample sizes; passing all four is what a
  real edge does and luck almost never does in one shot — but luck given
  ENOUGH shots passes anything. That is the whole problem this document
  governs.
- The validation gate (`trading/research.py`): ≥30 trades, permutation
  p < 0.05, bootstrap Sharpe 95% CI lower > 0, walk-forward majority
  profitable — enforced in code, reproducible by recorded seed.

## 2. The multiple-testing problem is the entire game

A permutation p < 0.05 gate passed once means little; the same gate passed
by candidate #47 of 200 tried means nothing at all — the expected number of
false passes under pure luck at p 0.05 over 200 candidates is 10. Any search
that does not account for its own size is curve-fitting with extra steps,
and the design doc says so explicitly about parameter optimization. This
program therefore treats **the count of everything tried as first-class
provenance**, recorded in a search ledger, and splits the data so that
selection and confirmation never share a bar.

The protocol:

1. **Data split, fixed in advance.** In-sample: everything before
   2025-11-01. Out-of-sample: 2025-11-01 → present (~13 months — never
   touched by any selection step, including "just looking at the equity
   curve"). The split date is fixed here, in writing, before the first
   candidate runs: changing it later is itself selection.
2. **Phase A — in-sample screening.** Candidates are evaluated on in-sample
   data with the FULL gate (all four checks, no shortcuts — a cheap screen
   that passes more candidates just moves the selection pressure downstream).
   Every candidate evaluated lands in the search ledger: family, parameters,
   verdict, the four statistics. Failures are recorded with the same care as
   passes — the count is the evidence.
3. **Phase B — out-of-sample confirmation, one shot each.** A candidate that
   passes phase A is run ONCE on out-of-sample data with the same gate.
   Pass → `validated` through the normal CLI path, and the search ledger
   row is part of its registration provenance. Fail → dead, permanently:
   it does not re-enter phase A with tweaked parameters, because that is
   the out-of-sample data leaking into selection through a human loop.
   (The family may spawn new candidates, but each is a new ledger entry —
   the count keeps growing, honestly.)
4. **Family budget.** Each strategy family gets a declared candidate budget
   before its first run. When the budget is spent, the family is closed.
   Budgets are the search's way of admitting it cannot try everything: the
   prior belief about where edges live decides the allocation, and the
   ledger shows what the belief cost.
5. **The final bar.** A strategy reaches `paper` only through the normal
   lifecycle — validation rows written by the unchanged CLI, never by the
   search machinery. The search proposes; the gate disposes. Nothing in
   this program weakens, bypasses, or parameterizes the gate.

Total budget: 216 candidates (156 declared at program writing + 20 for F7 + 20 for F2 + 20 for F8,

the sweep, never follows it). Under pure luck the expected false phase-A
passes at p 0.05 is ~8 — which is exactly why phase B exists: luck does not
repeat on demand, a real edge does. The expected phase-B false-pass rate per
candidate is the gate's joint false-positive rate, far below any single
test's 0.05 because the four tests fail independently for luck.

## 4. What a candidate IS

A python class implementing the same pure-strategy interface the catalog
uses (bars in, orders out — the harness owns execution, rails, sizing). One
parameter set = one candidate. Families may share regime/filter/sizing
machinery as library code; what may NOT happen is a candidate reading the
future, the gate's internals, or the out-of-sample split.

## 5. Work order

1. Data expansion (running): 10 symbols × {1h, 4h} × 1095 days into
   `research.db`. Gaps are provenance, not failure — the ledger records
   coverage per family evaluation. (Measured at launch: BTCUSDT 1h backfills
   to 2025-01 — 17 520 bars of the 26 280 requested; Binance USDT-M perp
   history or API paging is the ceiling, recorded not assumed.)
2. Search machinery: ledger schema (one sqlite table, append-only), the
   phase-A runner (parallel over cores, deterministic seeds), the phase-B
   runner (serial, one-shot, witnessed by the ledger).
3. F1 first: highest prior, simplest machinery, slowest data needs.
4. F2 needs funding-rate history ingestion — a new feed; estimated after
   F1's ledger teaches what the machinery costs in practice.
5. F3 needs the basket; the data is already landing.

## 6. What this program never does

- Never weakens the gate, the seed discipline, or the lifecycle — the search
  is a client of the validation path, not an editor of it.
- Never reports a phase-B pass without its ledger ancestry: how many failed
  before it is part of what the pass means.
- Never trades an unvalidated candidate, in paper or otherwise — that rule
  predates this program and survives it.
- Never optimizes a candidate against out-of-sample results, including by
  human-in-the-loop "one more tweak". Death is death.

## 7. Honest expectations

Most likely outcome: the budget is spent, nothing validates, and the ledger
documents 156 honest failures — which is itself the correct product of a
research program whose alternative is lying. The prior-ranked ordering means
the first PASS, if any exists in these families, arrives early. Either way
the system learns legibly: the ledger is the first research artifact this
repository keeps that cannot flatter us.## F9. ML return forecaster | budget 12

Gradient-boosted regression of forward returns on the harness's own causal
feature frame, embargoed walk-forward trained, strictly out-of-fold noise
estimate. The mechanism hypothesis: the tape's short-horizon conditional
structure is visible to gradient boosting when leakage is impossible by
construction — train on in-sample only, validate out-of-sample virgin.

Leakage discipline declared BEFORE the first sweep: features causal,
labels forward-looking used only in training, walk-forward splits with
an embargo of twice the label horizon, artifact trained on the in-sample

First measurement 2026-11-26 (BTCUSDT 1h, horizon 6): OOF corr +0.042 vs
naive ret_8 -0.002 — a small genuine skill exists; whether it survives
costs and gates is the family's question. 10 cells = 5 valid (entry_z, exit_z) pairs x 2 symbols; budget 12, 2 in reserve.

## F10. Alpha-zoo survivors | budget 40

The vendored factor zoo measured on the crypto 10-universe at 4h, horizon 6
(bench of 2026-11-26, docs/research): 28 of 292 alphas reach significance —
14 alive, 14 reversed — with mean |IC| ~0.08 at t-stat ~15. The survivors
are decorrelated across five themes (pairwise signature correlations
mostly < 0.2), so this is a library, not one signal in five disguises.
Equities at 1d show zero significant alphas — honestly reported as a
universe-power limit (6 assets cannot carry cross-sectional ranks), not
evidence the alphas are dead there.

Mechanism hypothesis: cross-sectional price/volatility structure in crypto
perpetuals is persistent enough that ranking the universe by these formulas
predicts relative forward returns; per-symbol z-scored entries capture the
time-series projection of that cross-sectional edge. What would falsify it:
the kernel verdicts reject every survivor cell, or in-sample alphas die
out-of-sample — either closes the family inside its budget.

Grid: 5 decorrelated survivors (gtja191_093 microstructure-alive,
gtja191_159 momentum-reversed, gtja191_126 reversal-alive,
gtja191_097 volatility-reversed, gtja191_081 volume-reversed) x entry_z
(1.5, 2.0) x 2 lead symbols x phases = 40 cells exactly. Inversion is
never a guess: reversed alphas trade inverted because the bench measured
the inversion, and the bench file is the cited evidence.

## F11. Alpha-ranked basket | budget 24

F10 measured the question wrong on purpose and the ledger owns the answer:
cross-sectional alphas per-symbol are noise (phase A: mean Sharpe -0.23,
0/20 validated, best cell p 0.81). But the bench's IC is a CROSS-SECTIONAL
measurement — it claims the alpha ranks the universe informatively, not
that any single symbol trends with its z-score. The honest instrument for
a cross-sectional edge is a cross-sectional position: long the top-ranked
assets, short or flat the bottom-ranked, rebalanced on a schedule. This is
F3's machinery with the alpha as the ranking signal instead of trailing
returns — same basket harness, different ranking truth.

Mechanism hypothesis: the relative-value structure the bench measured
(IC +0.08, t-stat 15) is real at the universe level; whether it survives
basket execution, costs and the next-open delay is the family's question.
What would falsify it: basket verdicts reject across the grid, or the
in-sample basket edge dies out-of-sample — F10's per-symbol failure already
proves the edge does NOT project into time-series, so a basket failure
closes the alpha program's directional claims entirely.

Grid: 3 decorrelated survivors (gtja191_093 microstructure-alive,
gtja191_126 reversal-alive, gtja191_159 momentum-reversed) x top-k (2, 3)
x rebalance (24, 48 bars) = 12 baskets x 2 phases = 24 cells. Long/flat
only in phase A — the basket harness shorts through the same reversal
mechanics but the first question is whether even the long side ranks.

## F12. Regime-conditional ML | budget 16

Declared morning of day 3, 2026-11-27, after three families died at exactly
the same wall. F9 measured genuine in-sample entry skill (random-entries
null p = 0.0) that died out-of-sample; F7 and F11 died at the same boundary.
Three independent families, one shape of death — either the edges never
existed (rejected: the instruments measured them) or the regime moved and
frozen models did not move with it. F12 is the test of the second hypothesis,
the only unresolved question in the program.

Mechanism hypothesis: the conditional structure the ML forecaster detects
is real but regime-local; rolling retraining tracks it, so the edge
persists across the boundary that kills frozen models. What would falsify
it: rolling-retrained models show no better out-of-sample persistence than
F9's frozen one — then decay is not adaptation lag but something structural
(changed microstructure, crowding), and the ML program closes entirely.

The honest design, declared before measurement:
  - at bar t the model retrains on the trailing window of W bars ENDING
    at t - h, where h is the label horizon — no forward-looking label ever
    overlaps the prediction boundary, embargoed by construction
  - retraining cadence C bars; between retrains the last model scores
  - predictions precomputed into a causal column, the kernel unchanged —
    same injection discipline as every family before
  - cost measured before declaring: fit 0.26s at W=2000, so a full grid
    is hours of CPU, not days; predictions cached as research artifacts
  - grid: W (2000, 4000) x C (24, 48) x 2 symbols = 8 cells x 2 phases
    = 16 exactly; entry_z fixed at 1.0, exit 0.5 — the family's question
    is retraining, not thresholds

## F13. Alpha-augmented ML | budget 12 — declared, machinery only until F12 reads

Declared morning of day 3 alongside F12. The alpha zoo's directional
program is closed (F10/F11), but the zoo measured real cross-sectional
structure in-sample — information that decayed as an entry signal may
still be worth something as a FEATURE: ML models decide how much weight
each input deserves, which is exactly the judgment the bench cannot make
for them. F9/F12 use twelve price/volatility features; F13 adds the five
decorrelated survivor alphas as causal columns and asks whether the
rolling forecaster's skill deepens.

Mechanism hypothesis: alpha values carry regime-relevant state the price
features miss; the rolling retrain adapts weights as regimes move. What
would falsify it: augmented models show no OOF-corr improvement over F12's
price-only baseline on the same segments — then the zoo is truly dead even
as input, and the vendored library closes entirely.

Dependency declared honestly: F13 sweeps ONLY after F12 reads. If F12
dies, the ML program's premise dies with it and F13 never runs — that is
why the budget sits at 12 and the machinery is built but idle.

## Amendment, 2026-11-27 — G and H families declared before any measurement

Declared under the corrected statistical instrument (stats-repair
verdict, same day). Both families are sized for power BEFORE the grid is
finalized: a cell that cannot reach its minimum-detectable-Sharpe line is
re-designed or dropped, because the ledger no longer accepts verdicts
that were uninterpretable when written.

### G-family: funding-carry with venue truth (budget 20 cells, phase A)

The structural transfer — the one mechanism in the zoo that is not a
price forecast. Preconditions (week 3-4 work, hard gates):

1. Perp klines ingested as the trading truth (`/fapi/v1/klines`) — the
   family does not run on spot series with perp funding bolted on.
2. The `venue` column migration complete; funding joins keyed to perp
   series only.

Design: ride the uncrowded side of the funding regime, deviation-timed
entries, 4h bars (not 1h — the power bar at 1h cannot be reached within
the window; 4h halves resolution needs and matches the funding
settlement cadence honestly). Falsification criterion, written before
measurement: if the best phase-A cell's entries-null confirmation p
exceeds 0.10, the mechanism is dead as timing, not as transfer — the
family closes and the funding edge question moves to pure carry capture
(hold-through-settlement, no timing) as a separate smaller declaration.

### H-family: multi-week trend on daily bars (budget 20 cells, phase A)

The most replicated anomaly in existence, at the horizon where the
measurement says information lives (GLD showed the strongest ML skill;
equities showed none). Universe: BTC/ETH daily, GLD/SLV/TLT daily if the
data plane carries them, 3y depth minimum. Design: time-series momentum,
lookbacks in weeks not hours, vol-scaled sizing. The honest expectation,
written before measurement: this is the program's best prior of a
tradeable edge AND its least unique direction — a validated H cell is a
worse-QuantConnect capability, valuable exactly in proportion to its
boringness.

### What neither family is allowed to do

No grid pruning that correlates with expected performance after the
first sweep row lands. No budget expansion without an amendment written
before the overflow cell runs. No phase B on cells below the power bar —
they ledger as `untestable`, and the family re-designs rather than
confirms noise.
